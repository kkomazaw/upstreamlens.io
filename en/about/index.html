About

What is Upstream Lens

Upstream Lens is an unofficial, individually-operated analytical media that analyzes discussions, design philosophies, and structural changes in open source upstream communities, organizing and sharing them in a format useful for enterprise and platform engineers to make informed decisions.

Our Position

This site clarifies the following positions:

  • This is an unofficial, individually-operated site not affiliated with any specific foundation or organization
  • We explicitly state our perspective without pretending neutrality
  • We focus on decision-making structures, not just technology choices
  • We provide thinking frameworks, not tool comparisons

Target Audience

  • Platform Engineers
  • Enterprise / Cloud Architects
  • Engineers interested in OSS community structures
  • Leads and managers involved in technology selection and standardization

What This Site Does NOT Provide

  • Beginner tutorials or hands-on guides
  • How-to guides for individual tools
  • Tool comparison rankings
  • News or breaking updates
